MLB Pitcher Arsenal Analysis

This project uses 2025 MLB starting pitcher data from the website Baseball Savant to investigate how a pitcher's pitch arsenal — the types and mix of pitches they throw — affects key performance metrics. Many Major League Pitchers are said to be one adjustment or one pitch away from being one of the best in the league and this project takes a deep dive into if that claim really holds true.
